Converge, made ours

Four refinements of Converge — all locked to exactly 3 source nodes (mirroring the product's real "3 sources"), all directional left→right, each trying a different focal-node treatment instead of the atom-ish halo ring.

Goal: get as far from the generic radial hub-and-spoke as possible. The asymmetry (sources on one side, you on the other) is the differentiator — these push it.
pick
V1

Diamond focal

24px

The focal node becomes an iris diamond — a deliberate "signal/flag" shape that echoes the relevance badge used across the UI.

Distinct: a diamond hub is unusual (most are round); ties directly to the product's flag motif. Sharp at favicon size.
V2

Solid

24px

No ring at all — just a single bold focal node, weight carrying the emphasis. The cleanest, most timeless option.

Distinct: drops the halo entirely; pure weight hierarchy. Reads instantly, ages well, hardest to misread.
V3

Aperture

24px

The focal node is a hollow ring with a pupil — an eye/aperture "focusing" on the incoming signals. Foresight, literally.

Distinct: the pupil makes it a "seeing" node, not an atom. Strongest meaning, slightly busier than V2.
V4

Lens

24px

A solid focal node with one outgoing arc — the signal is received and re-emitted: sources in, foresight out.

Distinct: a single trailing arc nods to the radar story without the RSS look — bridges Converge and Sweep.
